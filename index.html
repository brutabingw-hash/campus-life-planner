About

Why this exists

Campus Life Planner is a coursework project for the Building Responsive UI assignment. It is a single-page, framework-free app that keeps track of campus tasks and events, measures how much time they will take, and says something when a week gets overbooked.

How it works

  • Vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript ES modules, with no libraries.
  • Regular expressions validate every form field and power the activity search.
  • Data lives in localStorage and can be exported or imported as JSON.
